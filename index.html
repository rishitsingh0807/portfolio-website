About Me

Designing circuits,
building the future.

I'm Rishit Singh, an Electrical Engineer passionate about power systems, embedded hardware, and precision instrumentation. I thrive at the intersection of analogue design and digital control — where careful engineering meets real-world physics.

From high-voltage pulse generators to laser interferometers, I enjoy building things that push the boundaries of measurement and control. When I'm not at the bench, I write about engineering concepts and share what I learn along the way.
